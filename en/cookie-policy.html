    ARTICLE 1: In Short

    The website https://ezra-maroc.com (the "Site"), published by Ezra Maroc Consulting Services LLC ("Ezra Maroc", "we"), places no cookies on your device and uses no audience measurement, advertising or social media trackers (no embedded social media buttons or content). The only exception is on the Contact page: the form's anti-bot check, strictly necessary for its security (see article 4).

    That is why no banner asks for your consent: the regulations (GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive and the recommendations of data protection authorities such as the French CNIL) only require consent for non-essential cookies and trackers, which we do not use.

    ARTICLE 2: What Is a Cookie?

    A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) during your visit, in order to recognize it or remember certain information. The term "tracker" more broadly covers all equivalent technologies: browser local storage, invisible pixels, device fingerprinting, etc.

    ARTICLE 3: How the Site Works Without Cookies

    • Fonts and icons: they are hosted on the Site itself. Your browser does not contact any external service (such as Google Fonts) to display them.
    • No embedded content: the Site does not display any map, video or social media widget coming from another website.
    • Instagram link: it is a simple link. Instagram receives no information unless you click on it; once you are on Instagram, the policy of its publisher (Meta) applies.
    • Language choice: the language selector simply takes you to the corresponding page; your choice is not stored.

    ARTICLE 4: Contact Form

    When you send the contact form, the information you enter is transmitted to our service provider Cloudflare, which forwards it to us by email; Formspree takes over only if this service is temporarily unavailable. These transmissions do not require any cookie.

    To block automated submissions (spam), the Contact page uses the Cloudflare Turnstile anti-bot check, which is usually invisible. It places no cookies, but stores a technical value ("cf.turnstile.u") in your browser's local storage for the challenges.cloudflare.com domain, used solely for this check. It is not used for advertising or to track your browsing; as it is strictly necessary for the security of the form, it does not require your consent. The processing of this data is described in our Privacy Policy.

    ARTICLE 5: Hosting

    The Site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages service). As with any website, the host technically receives your IP address in order to send you the pages you request; this does not involve any cookie. The host's details are given in our Legal Notice.

    ARTICLE 7: Changes to This Policy
